\begin{abstract}
Autoregressive Causal Transformers suffer from four structural bottlenecks in continuous physical telemetry, symbolic conservation reasoning, and edge deployment: (1) an $\mathcal{O}(L^2)$ quadratic attention memory wall, (2) unconstrained violation of physical and algebraic conservation laws, (3) static post-deployment weights that cannot adapt online without global backpropagation, and (4) purely associative correlation rather than interventional $do$-calculus. We introduce the \textbf{Resonant Causal World Engine (RCWE)}, a non-autoregressive architecture uniting complex phase-space wavepackets, $\mathcal{O}(L \log L)$ spectral mixing with a strictly constant $\mathcal{O}(1)$ standing wavefield state ($|\Psi| = 8.0\text{--}16.0\text{ KB}$), reversible Symplectic Leapfrog Verlet integration on learned Hamiltonian manifolds, and backprop-free 3-factor metabolic synaptic plasticity with offline sleep consolidation. Dispatched via a custom Apple Metal Shading Language (\texttt{MSL}) kernel on Apple Silicon (\texttt{mps}), RCWE executes single-pass forward inference across $L = 131,072$ tokens in $32.22\text{ ms}$ (a $33,554,432\times$ state compression ratio over a $256\text{ GB}$ Transformer attention workspace), reduces multi-seed post-mutation MSE by $160.1\times$ ($0.00071$ vs.\ $0.11367$) and Hamiltonian energy drift by $8.94\times$, achieves $97.8\% \pm 3.8\%$ causal regime classification accuracy, neutralizes covert Byzantine sinkhole faults by $10.8\times$ in closed-loop self-healing control, and outperforms frozen Transformers by $73.4\times\text{--}472.4\times$ across ETT, SWaT, NASA Turbofan, and SCADA telemetry benchmarks.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
While Transformer architectures dominate static sequence modeling, their $\mathcal{O}(L^2)$ attention matrices and $\mathcal{O}(L)$ Key-Value caches become prohibitive over ultra-long horizons ($L \ge 32,768$). Furthermore, unconstrained residual layers routinely violate conservation laws in physical networks and double-entry symbolic ledgers, and frozen inference parameters fail under abrupt runtime topology mutations.

\subsection{Symplectic Causal World-Lines \& MDO Foraging}
Each world-line branch $k \in \{0, \dots, K-1\}$ parameterizes a scalar potential $V_k(q \odot m_{\text{do}}, a)$ and inverse mass matrix $M_k^{-1} = \text{softplus}(\theta_m)$, evolving canonical coordinates $(q_t, p_t)$ via second-order St\"ormer-Verlet Leapfrog steps:
\begin{align}
  p_{t+1/2} &= p_t - \frac{\Delta t}{2} \nabla_q V_k(q_t, a_t), \\
  q_{t+1} &= q_t + \Delta t \, (p_{t+1/2} \odot M_k^{-1}), \\
  p_{t+1} &= p_{t+1/2} - \frac{\Delta t}{2} \nabla_q V_k(q_{t+1}, a_t).
\end{align}
To resolve epistemic ambiguity across competing world-lines, RCWE evaluates candidate interventions $\{do(a^{(c)})\}$ against the baseline $do(0)$, selecting the \textbf{Minimum Distinguishing Observation (MDO)}:
\begin{equation}
  c^* = \arg\max_c \, \text{Var}_{k}\!\left[ \hat{y}_k(z_t, do(a^{(c)})) - \hat{y}_k(z_t, do(0)) \right].
\end{equation}
